# 02 — Business Strategy

## Market Positioning

NAVNA positions itself as a **modern digital partner** — not a freelance marketplace listing, not a massive agency. A small, technically skilled studio that delivers high-quality digital products directly.

### Positioning Statement
> For businesses that need digital products built with care, NAVNA is a modern web and technology studio that combines strategy, design, and development to deliver websites, applications, and digital systems that actually work.

## Revenue Model

- **Project-based:** Fixed-scope projects with clear deliverables
- **Retainer-based:** Ongoing development, maintenance, and improvement
- **Consultation:** Strategy and advisory for businesses planning digital work

## Competitive Landscape

### Competitors by Category

| Category | Examples | NAVNA Differentiator |
|----------|----------|---------------------|
| Large agencies | WPP, Deloitte Digital | NAVNA offers direct founder involvement, faster communication, lower overhead |
| Mid-size studios | Various regional studios | NAVNA offers modern tech stack, performance focus, competitive pricing |
| Freelancers | Individual developers/designers | NAVNA offers broader capability, reliability of a team, professional process |
| Template/DIY | Wix, Squarespace, WordPress themes | NAVNA offers custom-built solutions, unique design, technical depth |

### NAVNA Advantages
1. **Founder-led delivery** — Clients work directly with the people building their product
2. **Modern technology** — Next.js, React, TypeScript — not legacy stacks
3. **Full-spectrum** — Strategy + Design + Development under one roof
4. **Performance-first** — Every project optimized for speed and SEO
5. **Transparent process** — Clear communication, no black box

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Current)
- Launch navna.online
- Build portfolio with concept projects
- Begin content marketing via /insights
- Target local and remote clients

### Phase 2 — Traction
- Replace concept projects with real case studies
- Build referral network
- Expand service offerings based on demand
- Begin targeted outreach

### Phase 3 — Scale
- Hire selectively as demand grows
- Productize common service packages
- Build recurring revenue through retainers
- Establish thought leadership in chosen niches
