# 12 — Content Strategy

## Writing Principles

### Voice
Clear. Direct. Confident. Human.

### Rules

1. **Write for humans first** — Search engines reward good human writing
2. **Be concise** — Every word must earn its place
3. **Be specific** — "We build websites" > "We create innovative digital solutions"
4. **Be honest** — Never claim what isn't true
5. **Avoid jargon** — Unless addressing a technical audience specifically
6. **Active voice** — "We build" not "Solutions are built by us"
7. **Present tense** — "We design" not "We will design"

### Tone Calibration

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Hero/headlines | Bold, concise, memorable |
| Service descriptions | Clear, informative, benefit-focused |
| Process explanations | Structured, reassuring, transparent |
| About/founders | Warm, genuine, professional |
| FAQ answers | Direct, helpful, honest |
| CTA sections | Energetic, inviting, action-oriented |
| Legal pages | Professional, clear, standard |

## Page-by-Page Content Direction

### Homepage

**Hero Headline:** "Websites & applications built for what's next."
**Supporting text:** 1-2 sentences max. Must answer: What does NAVNA do? Who is it for?

**Sections to write:**
- Value proposition (4 pillars, 1 sentence each)
- Service cards (title + 1-sentence description each)
- Process steps (6 steps, 1 sentence each)
- Why NAVNA (6 differentiators, 1-2 sentences each)
- CTA (headline + button)

### Service Pages (7)

Each needs:
- **Headline:** What the service is (clear, not clever)
- **Problem:** 2-3 sentences about what businesses face
- **Solution:** 2-3 sentences about NAVNA's approach
- **Deliverables:** Bullet list of concrete outputs
- **Process:** How this service works specifically
- **FAQ:** 3-4 questions specific to this service

### About Page

- **Story:** 3-4 paragraphs. Keep it genuine. NAVNA was started by two developers who believe businesses deserve better digital products. No manufactured drama.
- **Mission:** 1 sentence.
- **Vision:** 1 sentence.
- **Values:** 4-5 core beliefs, 1 sentence each.
- **Founders:** Brief professional descriptions using publicly available information only.

### Contact Page

- **Headline:** "Start a Project"
- **Supporting text:** "Tell us about your project and we'll get back to you."
- Simple. No pressure. No fake urgency.

### FAQ Page

Direct answers. No filler.
- Q: "What does NAVNA do?" → A: "NAVNA designs and builds websites, web applications, and digital systems for businesses."
- Keep answers to 2-3 sentences maximum.

## Content to AVOID

| ❌ Don't Write | ✅ Write Instead |
|----------------|-----------------|
| "We are a passionate team of digital ninjas" | "We design and build digital products for businesses" |
| "Revolutionizing the digital landscape" | "Modern websites that perform well" |
| "Cutting-edge, world-class solutions" | "Custom-built websites using current technology" |
| "500+ satisfied clients" | (omit — don't have 500 clients) |
| "Award-winning design" | (omit — no awards yet) |
| "Your one-stop digital solution" | "Websites, applications, and automation — all from one team" |
