## Website Purpose

The NAVNA website serves as the primary digital storefront and trust engine for the agency. It must:

1. **Communicate** what NAVNA does — clearly and immediately
2. **Demonstrate** capability through design quality and portfolio
3. **Build trust** through transparency, real information, and professional presentation
4. **Convert** visitors into project inquiries via the contact form
5. **Rank** in search engines for relevant web development/design queries
6. **Be discoverable** by AI answer engines, generative search, and traditional search

---

## Business Goals

1. Establish NAVNA as a credible, modern digital agency
2. Generate inbound project inquiries through the website
