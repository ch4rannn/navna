# 05 — User Personas

## Persona 1 — Rahul (Small Business Owner)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Rahul Mehta |
| **Age** | 38 |
| **Role** | Owner, local accounting firm |
| **Location** | Pune, India |
| **Digital maturity** | Low — has a basic WordPress site from 2019 |

### Goals
- Get a professional website that builds credibility with potential clients
- Appear trustworthy and established when prospects search online
- Generate more inquiries through his website
- Improve his Google presence for local searches

### Pain Points
- Current website looks outdated and doesn't reflect his firm's quality
- Doesn't know how to evaluate web development services
- Has been burned by a cheap freelancer who delivered poor work and disappeared
- Worried about ongoing maintenance and costs

### Objections
- "How do I know you'll actually deliver what you promise?"
- "I don't have a huge budget — is this going to be worth the investment?"
- "Will I be able to make changes myself, or will I need you forever?"
- "My current website was supposed to be good too — what's different?"

### Buying Motivation
Needs to modernize his online presence to compete with larger firms that are winning clients through superior digital marketing.

### Decision Criteria
1. Portfolio quality — does their work look professional?
2. Clear process — can they explain exactly what will happen?
3. Fair pricing — value for money, not necessarily cheapest
4. Communication — are they responsive and clear?
5. Ongoing support — what happens after launch?

### NAVNA Solution
Professional business website with modern design, clear service presentation, contact integration, and basic SEO. Clean, fast, credible.

### Likely Website Journey
1. Finds NAVNA via Google search ("website design agency India")
2. Lands on homepage → reads hero → understands what NAVNA does
3. Views portfolio → sees quality of work
4. Reads process → understands what to expect
5. Checks about page → sees real people, builds trust
6. Reads FAQ → gets answers to concerns
7. Clicks "Start a Project" → fills contact form
