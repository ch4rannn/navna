# 04 — Target Audience

## Primary Audiences

### 1. Small Business Owners
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Business size** | 1–50 employees |
| **Revenue** | Early-stage to established |
| **Industry** | Service businesses, retail, hospitality, professional services |
| **Digital maturity** | Low to moderate — may have an outdated website or none at all |
| **Primary need** | Professional website that builds credibility and generates inquiries |
| **Budget range** | Moderate — looking for value, not the cheapest option |
| **Decision maker** | Owner or founder directly |

### 2. Startup Founders
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Business size** | 1–10 people |
| **Stage** | Pre-seed to Series A |
| **Industry** | Technology, SaaS, fintech, health tech, ed-tech |
| **Digital maturity** | Moderate to high — technically literate |
| **Primary need** | MVP development, landing page, SaaS application |
| **Budget range** | Varies — often constrained but willing to invest in quality |
| **Decision maker** | Founder or CTO |

### 3. Established Businesses
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Business size** | 50–500 employees |
| **Revenue** | Established, growing |
| **Industry** | Manufacturing, logistics, finance, healthcare, professional services |
| **Digital maturity** | Moderate — may have legacy systems and outdated websites |
| **Primary need** | Website redesign, digital transformation, automation |
| **Budget range** | Higher — willing to invest in long-term solutions |
| **Decision maker** | Marketing director, CTO, or operations lead |

### 4. Personal Brands / Creators
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Freelancers, consultants, coaches, artists, photographers |
| **Digital maturity** | Low to moderate |
| **Primary need** | Portfolio website, personal brand, lead generation |
| **Budget range** | Lower to moderate |
| **Decision maker** | The individual |

### Primary Markets
- **India** — Domestic market, primary initial focus
- **Remote global** — English-speaking markets accessible via remote work

### Secondary Markets
- **Southeast Asia** — Growing digital economy, similar cost advantages
- **Middle East** — Emerging tech hubs

---

## Psychographic Profile

### What They Value
- **Quality** — They want work that looks and works professionally
- **Communication** — They want to understand what's happening with their project
- **Reliability** — They want deadlines met and promises kept
- **Modern technology** — They want something built with current tools, not legacy systems
- **Results** — They care about business outcomes, not just pretty designs

### What They Fear
- Wasting money on a website that doesn't work
- Hiring a freelancer who disappears mid-project
- Getting a generic template passed off as custom work
- Being unable to update or maintain their website
- Poor performance hurting their search rankings

### How They Find Agencies
1. Google search ("web development agency," "website design")
2. Referrals from peers
3. LinkedIn network
4. Portfolio/case study discovery
5. Social media
6. Directory listings (Clutch, Dribbble, etc.)

---

## Decision Criteria

| Factor | Weight | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Portfolio quality | High | They want to see work quality before engaging |
| Clear pricing / process | High | They want to know what to expect |
| Communication quality | High | Responsiveness and clarity matter |
| Technical capability | Medium–High | Especially for technical audiences |
| Reviews / testimonials | Medium | Social proof matters, but portfolio matters more |
| Price | Medium | Important but not the sole factor for quality-seeking clients |
| Location | Low | Most clients are comfortable with remote work |
